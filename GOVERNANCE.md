# Governance

gapdrill is an open project. Anyone can contribute, and decisions are made in
public: in issues, pull requests and design docs.

## Maintainers

Maintainers are listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). They review and
merge changes, cut releases and look after the project's direction.

## Decisions

Most decisions are made by lazy consensus: a proposal goes ahead if no
maintainer objects within a reasonable time (at least three working days for
significant changes). If maintainers disagree and can't reach consensus, a
simple majority of maintainers decides.

Changes to the drill or report formats, or to how isolation works, need a
design doc in `docs/design/` and approval from a maintainer who didn't write
it, once the project has more than one maintainer.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who have made sustained, high-quality contributions (code,
reviews, docs or support) can be nominated by a maintainer. Nominations pass
with the approval of a majority of the current maintainers. Maintainers who
have been inactive for six months may be moved to emeritus status.

The project aims to have maintainers from more than one organization.

## Changing this document

Changes to this document need the approval of two-thirds of the maintainers.
